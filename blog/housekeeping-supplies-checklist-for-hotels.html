Housekeeping Supplies Checklist for Hotels

12 June 2026 · 4 min read · By the SLD team

Housekeeping purchases are small individually and significant together. A simple monthly checklist prevents last-minute buying at retail prices and keeps standards consistent across shifts.

Guest rooms

  • Toilet rolls and facial tissue boxes
  • Bin liners in room-bin size
  • Surface and glass cleaners

Public washrooms

  • Soap and tissue dispensers with refills
  • Toilet and floor cleaners
  • Medium garbage bags

Kitchen and restaurant

  • Kitchen towel rolls and table napkins
  • Food-handling gloves
  • Scrubbers, wipes and dish cleaners
  • Large and jumbo garbage bags

Back of house

  • Mops, brooms and buckets
  • Heavy-duty cleaning gloves
  • Floor cleaner in bulk cans

Three ways to control cost

First, standardise: one napkin, one toilet roll, one dispenser type. Second, track monthly consumption per occupied room so over-use shows up early. Third, combine housekeeping with your packaging order to save on delivery and paperwork.
