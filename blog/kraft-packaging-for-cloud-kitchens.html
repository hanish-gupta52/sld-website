Kraft Packaging for Cloud Kitchens: What to Stock

5 August 2026 · 4 min read · By the SLD team

A cloud kitchen has no dining room. The bag that arrives at the door is the entire brand experience, and brown kraft packaging gives it a clean, considered look at a reasonable cost.

A practical starter kit

  • Kraft bowls with lids in 500 ml and 750 ml for rice bowls and mains.
  • Noodle boxes for Indo-Chinese dishes and starters.
  • Burger boxes for burgers, sandwiches and wraps.
  • Salad bowls with clear lids for cold items.
  • Twisted-handle paper bags in two sizes.
  • Wooden spoons and forks, plus napkins.

Keep the range tight

Every extra pack size ties up cash and shelf space. Most kitchens can serve a full menu with five or six items. Fewer items also means bigger quantities of each — and better wholesale rates.

Brand it simply

Plain brown kraft takes a sticker or rubber stamp well. That is often enough to make an order feel branded without the minimum quantities of custom printing.

Watch cost per order

Add up bowl, lid, cutlery, napkin and bag for a typical order. Knowing this figure helps you price your menu and decide where a premium pack is worth it.
