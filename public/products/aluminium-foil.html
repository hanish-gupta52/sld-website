Buying guidance

How to choose

  • 250 ml for sides and sweets; 450 ml for single curry; 660–750 ml for single biryani.
  • 3300 ml half-deep trays suit family packs of 4–6 portions.
  • 9000 ml full-deep trays are for catering quantities.
  • Longer rolls (36 m, 72 m) cost less per metre for commercial kitchens.
