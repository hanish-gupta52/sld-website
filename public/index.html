Support

Frequently asked
questions

Quick answers on minimum orders, delivery, samples and billing. Still unsure? Call or WhatsApp us Mon–Sat, 9am–8pm.

01Is there a minimum order quantity?

No fixed minimum for retail purchases — you can buy a single pack. Wholesale rates generally apply from carton quantities. Share your list and we will tell you the best price break for each item.

02Which areas do you deliver to?

We deliver across Hyderabad and Secunderabad, and to major cities in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh including Warangal, Karimnagar, Nizamabad, Khammam, Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam, Guntur, Nellore and Tirupati. Delivery terms depend on order size and location.

03Can I get samples before ordering in bulk?

Yes. For most products we can share samples so you can test them with your own food before committing. Tell us the items you are considering and we will arrange it.

04Do you provide a GST invoice?

Yes. Every order is billed with a proper GST invoice, and any mix of categories can go on a single invoice for easier accounting.

05How does bulk pricing work?

Pricing is slab-based: the larger the quantity per item, the lower the rate. Regular monthly buyers can discuss a fixed rate list so prices stay predictable.

06Do you offer custom sizes or special requirements?

We stock a wide range of standard sizes and can source specific sizes on request. Availability and lead time vary by product — ask us and we will confirm before you order.
