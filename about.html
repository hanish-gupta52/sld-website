for 24+ years

A family-run distributor that grew with the city's food businesses — one carton, one customer and one honest recommendation at a time.

Our story

Built on repeat orders

Sri Laxmi Distributor began in Hyderabad as a small supplier of disposable packaging to nearby eateries, sweet shops and stores. The idea was simple: keep the right stock, charge a fair price and deliver when promised.

That idea has not changed. What has changed is the scale. Today we stock more than 2,000 products across seven categories — from bagasse plates and paper cups to Freshwrapp aluminium foil and housekeeping material — and serve over 5,000 customers across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

Most of our business comes from customers who order again. We take that as the clearest measure of whether we are doing the job properly.

Our history

How we got here

  1. The beginning

    Founded in Hyderabad

    Sri Laxmi Distributor opens as a small packaging supplier serving local eateries, sweet shops and kirana stores.

  2. Growing up

    Wholesale expansion

    Demand from restaurants and caterers leads to a larger stock range and carton-level wholesale supply.

  3. Going further

    Two-state delivery

    Doorstep delivery extends beyond Hyderabad to cities across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

  4. A new focus

    Eco-first range

    Bagasse tableware and brown kraft packaging become a core part of the catalogue as customers move away from single-use plastic.

  5. Today

    2,000+ products, 5,000+ customers

    Seven categories, authorised Freshwrapp distribution and a team that still answers every enquiry personally.
