Buying guidance

How to choose

  • Size garbage bags to your bins — oversized bags waste money.
  • Keep separate gloves for food handling and cleaning.
  • Standardise dispensers so refills are interchangeable.
  • Plan a monthly list; housekeeping items are steady and predictable.
