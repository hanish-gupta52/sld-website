Buying guidance

How to choose

  • Pick bowl capacity by portion weight: 500 ml suits most single rice bowls.
  • Use clear lids when presentation sells the dish; paper lids for hot food.
  • Choose bag size by your largest regular order.
  • Add wooden cutlery to stay consistent with plastic-ban rules.
