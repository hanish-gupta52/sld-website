Buying guidance

How to choose

  • Match size to serving: 65–100 ml tea, 150 ml coffee, 250 ml+ for takeaway drinks.
  • Choose double wall or ripple for very hot drinks carried by hand.
  • Order lids with the cups — diameters differ between makes.
  • For cold drinks, ask for cups suited to condensation.
