Bagasse Tableware

Bagasse is the fibre left after sugarcane is crushed for juice. Pressed into tableware, it makes plates and boxes that hold hot, oily and wet food without going soft — and that compost after use. As a bagasse plates supplier in Telangana, we stock the full range for dine-in, takeaway and events.

Buying guidance

How to choose

  • Choose plate size by menu: 6–7 inch for snacks, 10–12 inch for meals.
  • Use compartment plates or trays when gravies and dry items travel together.
  • For delivery, pick trays or clamshells with a matching lid rather than loose plates.
  • Very thin gravies held for long hours do better in a bowl with a lid.
